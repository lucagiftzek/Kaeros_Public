## Page purpose

Kaeros Help Center — answers to common questions about the CV writing process, payments, revisions, and delivery.

## Key Kaeros context

- Kaeros is a tech-enabled career services platform focused on human-written, HR-reviewed CVs.
- The workflow combines structured intake, expert writing, and an HR quality-assurance layer.
- Services are available in Greek, Italian, and English.
- The near-term focus is Greece and Europe, with Italy/EU expansion and wider international support.
- Kaeros does not use AI to write CVs — all writing is done by qualified human writers.
- Payment is completed before the writing work starts. Price and package are shown clearly before confirmation.
- All services are one-off payments. There are no subscriptions.
